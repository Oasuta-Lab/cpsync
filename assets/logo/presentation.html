What we heard

The brief

A self-hosted, open-source clipboard relay for developers and power users: copy on the phone, it lands on the desktop within seconds. Lives in the system tray. It should feel instant, light, private and multi-device, without the category clichés (clipboard clip, circular sync arrows, cloud).

instantlightprivatemulti-devicequiet

Reads as phone -> computer at a glance, survives 16-22 px as a tray icon in one colour, distinct from blue dev-tool marks.

Final design

Phone to laptop

The piece cut out of the phone has landed inside the laptop: solid and hollow mirror each other, and the orange piece is the clipboard content in transit.

  • Phone -> laptop reads at a glance, including at 16 px
  • Hole and piece face each other across the gap, so the link is visible without arrows
  • One colour works for the tray; the orange piece carries the brand in colour
  • Rounded-rectangle geometry on an 8-unit grid, one radius system
