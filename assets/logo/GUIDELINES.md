# CpSync — Logo Guidelines (compact)

## 1. The logo
- **Idea**: the piece cut out of the phone has landed inside the laptop. Solid and hollow mirror each other; the orange
  piece is the clipboard content in transit.
- **Versions**: horizontal (primary) · stacked · symbol-only · wordmark-only · app icon (ink tile).
- **Files**: masters in `assets/logo/*.svg`, generated by `python3 assets/logo/build.py`. Delivery variants
  (black, white, mono, PNG, favicon + web icon set) in `assets/logo/export/`.
  Use `cpsync-symbol-small.svg` (or the `favicon*` files) below 32 px.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **1 × the height of the orange piece** around the logo on all sides. The zone scales with the
logo; never use a fixed distance.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 80 px wide | 20 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (use the small-size files) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone (closest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | #14161F | 20 22 31 | 35 29 0 88 | Black 6 C |
| Signal | #FF6A3D | 255 106 61 | 0 58 76 0 | 1655 C |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | — |

**Approved pairs**: ink + signal on white · white + signal on ink · one colour (black, white or ink) anywhere else.
The orange is only ever the piece; never fill the devices or the wordmark with it.
In the tray the whole mark takes the connection-state colour (grey / green / red); that is a functional exception, not
a brand colour.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark: Adwaita Sans Bold (opsz 32), tracking −2.5 %, outlined to paths. Do not retype it.
- UI and documents: Adwaita Sans / Inter, fallback `system-ui, sans-serif`.
- Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (logo use permitted).

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch or squash · don't recolour outside the palette · don't rotate · don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or
effects · don't move the piece or the hole · don't rebuild the lockups by hand · don't place on busy backgrounds
without the ink tile.

## 7. Notes
- Trademark clearance was not checked. Run a search (EUIPO/USPTO, reverse image search) before any commercial use.
- Masters are SVG only. Export PDF/EPS from the SVG if a printer asks for them.
